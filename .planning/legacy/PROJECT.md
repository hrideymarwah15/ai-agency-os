# AI Agency OS — PROJECT ATLAS

## Project Overview

**Name:** AI Agency OS  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Type:** Autonomous AI-Powered Digital Agency Platform  
**Status:** Active Development  

## Vision

Create a fully autonomous AI-powered digital agency capable of finding leads, qualifying prospects, generating personalized outreach, negotiating within predefined limits, creating contracts, delivering projects, collecting payments, and managing clients with minimal human intervention.

The owner defines:
- Services
- Pricing rules
- Brand guidelines
- Legal templates
- Approval limits

The AI handles everything else.

## Core Value Proposition

An AI company that operates like a real agency with specialized departments instead of a single AI agent. Every project generates recurring monthly revenue wherever possible.

## Autonomous Workflow Pipeline

```
Daily Scheduler → Market Scanner → Lead Collector → Business Analyzer → Opportunity Ranking
    ↓
Personalized Audit → Proposal Generator → Negotiation Agent → Contract Generator → Payment Collection
    ↓
Project Creation → Development → Testing → Deployment → Maintenance → Upsell → Referral Request
```

## Compliance & Safety

- Respect website robots.txt and platform terms
- Rate-limit crawling and outreach
- Avoid unsolicited bulk email (anti-spam compliance)
- Explicit approval required for contracts, payments, commitments outside pricing rules
- Auditable log of every AI decision and action
